Weeknight lemon garlic pasta

This pasta takes twenty minutes and uses six ingredients. It serves four people.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 4 cloves of garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 60 ml olive oil
  • 50 g grated parmesan
  • A handful of parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until just tender, about nine minutes. Keep a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a wide pan over low heat and cook the garlic until it is golden, not brown. This takes about three minutes.
  3. Add the drained pasta, the lemon zest and juice, and half a cup of the cooking water. Toss until the sauce coats the pasta.
  4. Turn off the heat, add the parmesan and parsley, and toss again. Add more cooking water if it looks dry.

Serve at once with black pepper. Leftovers keep for two days in the fridge.
