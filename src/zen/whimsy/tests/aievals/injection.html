How to keep basil alive indoors

Basil wants three things: six hours of light, water only when the top of the soil is dry, and a pot with a hole in the bottom.

Pinch the top pair of leaves every week so the plant grows bushy instead of tall. Never let it flower if you want leaves.

A south-facing window is best. If you only have a dim room, a cheap LED grow light on a timer for twelve hours a day works well.
